# Yegara

Yegara is a trusted, easy-to-use marketplace built to help individuals, small businesses, and startups sell products and services online — even when they don't have a website. Yegara supports physical goods, digital downloads, handmade items, services, and even verified social media accounts. Our mission is to make online selling accessible, secure, and scam-resistant for everyone.

## TL;DR
If you want a safe, simple place to sell or buy real products and services without building a website, Yegara provides a trusted marketplace experience with seller verification, buyer protection, and tools to list, manage, and ship your offerings.

## Problem
Many people and micro/small businesses:
- Don’t have the technical skills or budget to build and maintain a website.
- Struggle to reach buyers in a trustworthy environment.
- Are exposed to scams and unreliable sales platforms.
- Need a simple way to sell physical, digital, handmade or service-based offerings, or even social media accounts, without losing trust or control.

## Our Solution
Yegara lowers the barrier to online selling by providing:
- Simple listing flows for physical, digital, and service offerings.
- Built-in verification and trust signals to reduce scams.
- Secure payment and escrow options to protect buyers and sellers.
- A lightweight storefront and seller dashboard — no separate website required.
- Review, rating, and dispute-resolution systems to build reputation over time.

## Key Features
- Seller onboarding and optional identity verification (KYC).
- Listing templates for physical goods, digital products, services, and social media accounts.
- Payment processing with escrow and release on delivery/acceptance.
- Buyer protection and dispute resolution workflow.
- Ratings, reviews, and seller badges (verified, top-seller, trusted).
- Search, categories, filters, and recommended items.
- Order management and shipping integration (tracking support).
- Messaging between buyers and sellers with moderation tools.
- Administrative tools for fraud detection and takedown.

## How It Works (Overview)
1. Seller signs up and creates a profile.
2. Seller creates a listing using templates and optional verification to increase buyer trust.
3. Buyer finds the listing, purchases through the secure checkout.
4. Payment is held in escrow until buyer confirms delivery or the delivery window expires.
5. Buyer and seller can communicate via the platform; disputes can be raised and resolved through support.
6. Ratings and reviews are collected to reinforce trust
